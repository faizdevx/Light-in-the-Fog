# 🌿 Light in the Fog

> "A small light is enough to drive away a lot of darkness."  
> — Your quiet digital corner for peace, hope, and purpose.

---

## ✨ What is this?

**Light in the Fog** is a calming website made for those days when you feel lost, stuck, or a little hopeless.  
It greets you with a beautiful background, peaceful sounds, and a gentle button —  
tap it, and you’ll receive a random verse from the **Quran**, **Bible**, or **Bhagavad Gita**.

This is a small reminder from different faiths and philosophies that you're not alone.

---

## 🔮 Features

- 🎥 Aesthetic video background 
- 🎧 Peaceful ambient voice/music
- 🕊️ One-click verse from multiple holy books
- ✨ Glassmorphism + smooth UI
